The U.S. Patent and Trademark Office has granted Patent No. US12636339 to Darling Ingredients' health brand Rousselot for Nextida GC, a first-of-its-kind collagen peptide ingredient. Nextida GC was developed to help support healthy after-meal blood sugar levels naturally.
